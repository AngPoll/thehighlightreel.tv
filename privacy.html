Privacy Policy

This page describes the free demo as it works today. Update it if the product later stores data anywhere else.

Accounts stay in the browser

In this version, accounts are stored locally in the browser. When you create an account, this site saves a name, email address, salted password hash, and favorites in local storage on that device. This version does not send that account to a Highlight Reel server, and it does not sell personal information.

What a session is

Signing in keeps a session id in local storage so the demo can reopen your account on this device. Signing out removes the session id. The saved account remains on the device until you delete it in Settings or clear the site's browser data.

Video hosts

Playing a clip loads a player from the video host, such as YouTube. That host can collect information under its own policy, which this demo does not control.

How to remove what this version stored

  • Delete the account from Settings while you are signed in on that browser.
  • Or clear this site's local storage and site data in the browser.

Children

The demo is not directed at children. Do not create an account for a child.
