How it works

Three steps, then the feed. Newest clips stay at the top. Search is for the thing you actually remember: a team, a player, a date, a final.

Name, email, and a password. That is the whole form.

02

03

One continuous feed, newest first. The latest clip plays on the page. Everything else opens in a pop-up and leaves your place intact.

What you get

Official league highlights

Clips come from league channels, with the titles those channels published. No invented reels.

Favorites fill the My teams menu. Choose a club, a league, or All highlights. A team tag on a card filters the same way.

Geo-blocking leagues are checked where you are. A blocked clip gets a plain explanation, and you can hide those videos.
